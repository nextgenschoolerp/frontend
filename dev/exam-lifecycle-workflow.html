Exam lifecycle, live

One exam term from set-up to the end of the year: subjects, marks, the deadline lock, an unlock ticket and correction, results, progress cards, then promotion. Each step runs against the real services as the staff member who owns it.

School, class and exam year

The run creates its own term [workflow-demo] … in the chosen year and three demo students in the class, so seeded terms and marks stay unchanged. An empty section (B–E) keeps seeded classes out of it; in a section with students the teacher fills their marks in the demo term too, so results can be published.

Staff for each workflow role

Staff of the selected school from the authservice directory (this campus first). Each role is pre-filled with someone whose roles allow every call it makes. Check permissions signs each one in once and verifies their roles against what each endpoint allows.
